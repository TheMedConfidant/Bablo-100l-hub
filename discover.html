Bablo Hub™ — frequently asked questions

A quick introduction to the platform and its learning resources.

What is Bablo Hub™?

Bablo Hub™ is a student learning platform by TheMedConfidant, created to bring course resources and academic practice tools together for Bowen University students.

What can students use Bablo Hub™ for?

Students can access available course notes, flashcards, question banks, quizzes, CBT practice and practical learning resources.

Who is Bablo Hub™ designed for?

The platform is designed with Bowen University students in mind, including students in the College of Health Sciences.

How do I access Bablo Hub™?

Open the platform from the button below. Sign-in or account creation options are available within the app.
