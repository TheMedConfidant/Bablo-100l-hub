Student tools

Who is Bablo Hub for?

Bablo Hub is a Bowen University student learning platform designed for CoHES students. It brings Bowen University study resources, course questions and structured revision tools into one student-focused space, with resources that can grow as students progress through their programmes.

Who created it?

Bablo Hub was created by Bababola Moses Bolawaife, founder of TheMedConfidant Channel, using AI-assisted development to address practical study needs experienced by a medical student.
