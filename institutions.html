How resource mapping works

Source-aware content

Every resource should retain its origin and be mapped to the curriculum it supports. Shared foundational material may be reused only when the academic mapping supports it.

No silent cross-institution substitution

If a matching resource is not available for a student’s institution, programme or level, Bablo Hub should show an honest availability state rather than display an unrelated university’s material.

Availability is explicit

The institution-aware architecture is being expanded in stages. The presence of an institution in the platform’s selection flow does not imply an official partnership, endorsement or complete course catalogue.
