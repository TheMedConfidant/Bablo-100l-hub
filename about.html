THE STORY BEHIND THE PLATFORM

Built from a real student need.

Babalola Moses Boluwatife

I am Babalola Moses Boluwatife, known as Bablo. I grew to like the nickname in my secondary school, and I still bear it today. I am the founder, CEO and MD of TheMedConfidant Channel. I saw the gap long before I got there. My journey into MBBS was not a simple hurdle to pass through, and these challenges shaped who I am today. Without them, TheMedConfidant would not have been made.

Bablo Hub began as an MBBS programme hub, meant to grow from 100 Level through to graduation and to solve some of the study problems of preclinical students. I did not know how to code, so I turned to artificial intelligence. I do not depend on AI entirely for code, but I have found that it cuts the time needed to study a material. It almost feels like a cheat code.

People often asked how I could do these things while studying MBBS. My dream had always been in technology, and, as destiny would have it, here I am.

AI helps a great deal when you study, but it cannot replicate or explain medical learning the way someone who has been through a medical course can. I faced many limitations along the way, but I kept pressing on. My first project was my motivation to get into medical school. I still look at what I can achieve and those I can help with this journey, birthed by challenges and orchestrated by God.

Whenever you want to give up on your dreams or give in to external pressure, remember that I, Bablo, built this as a medical student. Anyone can do anything.
