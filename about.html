THE STORY BEHIND THE PLATFORM

Built from a real student need.

Babalola Moses Boluwatife

I am Babalola Moses Boluwatife, known as Bablo. I grew to like the nickname in secondary school, and I still bear it today. I am the founder of TheMedConfidant Channel.

My journey into medicine was not a simple hurdle to pass through. The challenges I encountered shaped my perspective and helped me recognise how much clarity, structure and accessible learning support matter to students.

Bablo Hub began as an MBBS programme hub, intended to grow from 100 Level through graduation and to solve some of the study problems faced by preclinical students. I did not know how to code, so I began using artificial intelligence as a practical tool to help turn ideas into working software. I still believe that the student’s understanding and judgement must guide the work.

People often asked how I could pursue these projects while studying medicine. My interest in technology had been there long before the platform. TheMedConfidant and Bablo Hub grew from the desire to use that interest to make learning clearer and more organised.

AI can assist learning and building, but it cannot replace the perspective of someone who has worked through a demanding course. I faced limitations along the way, but kept pressing forward. I remain grateful to God for the journey, the lessons and the people this work may help.

Whenever you feel pressured to abandon a worthwhile dream, remember that a student can begin with limited resources, learn along the way and build something useful. Start with what you have, and keep improving it.
