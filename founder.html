Founder of Bablo Hub™

Babalola M.B.

Founder and creator of Bablo Hub™

I am a medical student and education enthusiast interested in making learning more organised, connected and meaningful. Bablo Hub began with a question: could some of the challenges students face in medical education be made easier to navigate?

The inspiration

Before I received admission to study Medicine and Surgery (MBBS), there was a period when I almost lost interest in pursuing the course I had once been so passionate about. I began reflecting on why I had chosen Medicine in the first place. That reflection led me to another question: what problems could I solve within medical education?

I had noticed how frequently medical students raised concerns about one challenge or another. I began wondering whether there was a way to make their academic experience more organised and less stressful.

At the time, I had joined several MBBS-oriented groups and channels. Through one of them, I came across a post by Dr Nemo about Medking. That discovery further encouraged me to build something of my own: a platform that could be more specialised, more comprehensive and useful in its own right.

The journey

My route into Medicine was not straightforward. After graduating from secondary school, I did not gain admission immediately. I later pursued A-levels in Nigeria, hoping to move closer to my goal, but I still did not meet the required points.

By God's grace, I persevered. In 2026, I began the MBBS journey I had contemplated for so long. I wanted to turn some of the ideas and concerns I had observed into something practical, not only for myself but for other students navigating similar academic experiences.

The challenges of building

I began without a background in coding and without all the tools I would have preferred. There were days when progress felt slow, when things did not work as expected, and when the project seemed difficult to bring to life. There were also times when I could see how certain tools might help but could not justify the cost.

Still, I kept learning, experimenting, reconsidering ideas and building. What began as an attempt to solve a problem gradually grew into a broader educational vision.

The vision

I do not see Bablo Hub™ as merely a place for past questions, notes and quizzes. I envision a connected learning environment that can bring together doctors, medical students and other health-sciences students across Nigerian universities.

I want it to support research and academic collaboration; help students develop confidence in their knowledge and understanding; encourage learning beyond formal curricula; and make room for relevant discoveries, developments and educational news.

Ultimately, I want Bablo Hub™ to become a place where people can learn, connect, discover and grow.

Acknowledgements

Bablo Hub is not the product of one person's effort alone. Many people believed in the idea, encouraged me and helped make its development possible. I also acknowledge Dr Nemo's post about Medking as one of the inspirations that helped shape my thinking. To everyone who supported the vision in one way or another, thank you.

A message behind the journey: Your circumstances should inform your strategy, not determine the limits of your ambition. It is never too late to wake up and pursue the things you believe in. You cannot chase a dream while still sleeping.

— Babalola M.B.
