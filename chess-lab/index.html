CHESS ACADEMY

Study the board.
Own the next move.

A compact learning path designed around practical play.

01 · FOUNDATIONS

Board & piece movement

Learn ranks, files, diagonals and how each piece travels. Control the centre and develop pieces before launching an attack.

02 · KING SAFETY

Check, mate & stalemate

A king in check must be saved. Checkmate means no legal escape; stalemate is a draw when the player to move has no legal move but is not in check.

03 · TACTICS

Forks, pins & discovered attacks

Look for forcing moves: checks, captures and threats. A fork attacks multiple targets at once; a pin limits a piece because moving it exposes something more valuable.

04 · ENDGAMES

Convert an advantage

Activate your king, create passed pawns and calculate promotion races. In rook endgames, activity often matters more than holding a passive extra pawn.
